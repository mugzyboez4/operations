Why groups, not gut checks

Ceilings, set once

Every artist sits in a roster group — G1 Flagship down to G5 Emerging — each with a ceiling set with Ian from RCA historical actuals. Same yardstick, whoever's asking.

Why the gauge, not a debate

The verdict is computed

Spent + this request vs. ceiling — green with room, yellow inside the last 10%, red over. Fleck sees the same math every time, not a feeling.

Why blanket + pro-rate

Nothing gets lumped

Budgets spanning multiple vendors or deliverables pro-rate into their own PO line each — one Blanket PO, fully broken out, instead of five untracked ones.
