About this function

Campaign Ops sits between departments as a translation and routing layer. The work is tracking, surfacing, flagging, prompting, and confirming, so that information reaches the people who need it.

We do not approve, decide, or sit above any department. Where a page here shows your team, it is so other people can find you.

If something on these pages looks wrong, it probably is. Tell us and we will correct it.

Campaign Operations launched in March 2026. These pages are early and still changing shape alongside the departments they cover.
