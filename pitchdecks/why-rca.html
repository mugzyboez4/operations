Fixedstructure + the show-don't-tell rule

Names carry credits, arcs carry numbers, worlds carry proof. If a line states a value ("we develop," "we're global"), it gets cut and replaced with the receipt.

Exec grid + credits, four arc tiles, world wall order. A tailoring job, not a rebuild.
