Pitch Deck Bones

Six decks, one skeleton. Click anything to open it.v2 · 6 of 6 decks

Every deck is the same animal in different clothes. Nine bones, always in this order. Design is bespoke; the bones never change.

01Coverartist × RCA, in their world

Artist x RCA lockup in the artist's visual world. Sets the tone: the deck is their world, RCA-built.

02Why RCA / The Familythe boilerplate slot

Team intro, exec grid, the Sony system. Laufey shows the full form: eleven named execs. This is the "Why RCA" boilerplate slot — 2–3 pages, textable, top of every deck. Draft copy lives in the companion doc.

03The Thesiswhy this artist, why now

One bespoke slide in narrative prose. The A&R "story" input from the 2-slider drops straight in. In renewal decks (Variant D) it expands into a data story: what's working → what's missing.

04Goalsthree, numbered, verb-first

Måneskin: Activate Consumption / Expand The Movement / Fuel The Fandom. RoseHill: Sonic Identity / Extend The World / Drive Consumption. The template confirms 3-goal structure as house style.

05Campaign Tentpoles4–5 named big swings

Each expanded on its own slide. The most bespoke section — where the deck earns its keep. (Template: "Strategic Tent Poles.")

06Timelinesetup → core → post

SETUP → CORE CAMPAIGN (6–10 wks) → POST-RELEASE. Established artists get real dates; developing artists a 12–18 month horizon. Laufey's commerce frame: "BUILD FAST, LAND LOUD" — sub-3-month single-to-album window, DSPs briefed 3+ months out.

07Department Modulesthe repeatable middle — see Modules tab

Divider + 1–3 slides per department. Recurring order: Audience Dev → Digital → Advertising → Commercial → Physical/D2C → Publicity → Radio → Brands → Licensing → International. Fourteen modules in the library.

08RCA In Action2–4 case studies — see Proof tab

The same handful reused constantly, numbers refreshed from Laufey. Pre-packaged in the Proof library.

09Closingpersonality, never a summary

"<3 rca records" · template: "a welcome home for ARTIST NAME."

The bottleneck is never design — it's content for bones 03–07. Pre-build the modules and a deck becomes an assembly job, not a fire drill.

Fourteen modules. Pre-build once, swap the artist in. Each is a candidate for a designed master — the Part B plan from the 8/4 meeting.

Audience Developmentreach numbers · first-party data · fan funnel
  • Headline reach number (Måneskin: 200M+ pre-release impressions, broken down by source)
  • First-party data plan: email/SMS capture, retargeting pools
  • Platform-by-platform presence; for new acts, digital identity from zero pre-launch
  • Fan funnel: launch → capture → build → seed (street teams) → surface
  • Standing insight slides: age/gender split, streams by market
Digital / CreatorUGC · seeding · community CRM
  • Creator campaigns + UGC volume plays; platform-native seeding (fan pages, subreddits, memes)
  • Laufey's named cycle: Audience Data → Fan Pages → Influencers → Seeding → Clipping/UGC
  • Community CRM: Discord/fan-club funnel to VIP (Nina: 4–5K → VIP tiers)
  • Influencer lanes matched to the artist's world (RoseHill: BookTok; Nina: gaming)
  • In-character / in-world collabs where the concept allows
AdvertisingMarquee · paid on organic · lookalikes
  • Spotify Marquee + Showcase (Måneskin: ~24M-listener ecosystem)
  • TikTok/Meta paid layered on proven organic
  • Lookalike audiences off named comp artists; retargeting off owned data
Commercial / DSPper-platform partner asks
  • Per-platform pitch: Spotify, Apple, Amazon, YouTube, Meta — template has detailed on/off-platform ask lists
  • Partner Support Pitches standing slide: YouTube / IG / TikTok / Snapchat / Reddit / Discord / Facebook
  • VEVO DSCVR, COLORS, TikTok Elevate, Tiny Desk for developing acts, with precedent artists named
  • "Informed decision making" — data feedback loop framing
Physical / D2Cvariants · retail · superfan product
  • Staggered variant rollout; themed D2C-exclusive drops as first-party data capture (Laufey)
  • Retail: Amazon/Walmart/Target/B&N exclusives, in-store moments, pop-ups
  • Low-entry + collectible superfan balance
  • Product-concept slide: die-cut vinyl inserts, punch-out zines (template)
Publicitybroadcast ladder · named target lists
  • Two lanes: performance (morning TV → daytime → late night → digital → specialty → awards) and editorial (print/online + podcasts)
  • Template carries the master lists: Music, Fashion & Entertainment, Personality & Video, Podcasts, TV & Performance
  • "More With Less" posture for established acts (Laufey): fewer, bigger brand-forward moments + element of surprise
Radioformat claims · networks · headroom math
  • Format claim ("RCA dominated Alternative and Rock in 2025 — 10 #1 singles")
  • Single campaign: NY/LA/national syndication, named shows; "clean-slate next single" for renewals
  • Networks (iHeart, Audacy, SiriusXM, Cumulus, Townsquare) + events (Jingle Ball, iHeart Festival, We Can Survive)
  • The headroom argument (Laufey): Myles Smith 41.1M radio audience / 2.1M ODA vs. Laufey 2.6M / 2.0M — similar streams, 16x radio gap. Reusable for any under-radioed artist.
Brand Partnershipsbrand lanes · experiential
  • Per-artist brand lanes matched to identity — template spread: Sony Electronics, Crunchyroll, Hinge, Sims, Urban Outfitters, Fortnite
  • Experiential: pop-ups, café takeovers, studio-as-venue, mini festivals + masterclasses (Laufey: "A Very, Very Laufey Day", Cherry Lane residencies)
  • Named existing relationships (La Colombe, Postmates, Uber Eats) as proof of reach
Licensing / Synctargeted B2B · three lanes
  • "Sync success does not stem from passive discovery — it is a targeted B2B discipline"
  • Three-lane structure now standard: Prestige/Bespoke film · YA-focused sync · Lifestyle & Cultural (cozy games, Simlish, Fortnite/Roblox concerts)
  • Named target lists kept current per artist world
  • Bespoke placements as precedent (SZA "Hoppers", H.E.R., Red Clay Strays)
International50+ markets · localized execution
  • Sony global network — 50+ markets, locally relevant execution
  • Global DSP + Filtr playlisting; over-index geography slide (Laufey: Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Canada, Brazil, India)
  • Global creator strategy with localized assets
  • Events + ATL: listening events, landmark OOH, projections
  • Named outlets per territory (UK/Canada/Australia)

  • Superstar Collaborations slide: 22-name wishlist keyed produce/remix/feature (Doja → KATSEYE)

Creative — Personnel & WorldNewphotographers · directors · moodboards

One skeleton, four postures. Pick the posture first — it decides which modules go heavy.

AEstablished / Re-signingTeddy · Måneskin — "the machine at full power"
  • Lead with the family + Sony system — relationship and scale
  • Heavy on dominance stats and named partner leverage
  • Timeline has real dates tied to the next album cycle
BDeveloping ArtistNinajirachi — "help me make this world"
  • Lead with belief in the artist's world; development stories early
  • Tentpoles creative/community-first (listening parties, mixtapes, UGC, stems)
  • 12–18 month horizon through award-cycle ambitions
  • Partner-support pitches matter more than dominance stats
CPartnership / SoundtrackRoseHill — IP world-building
  • Goals reframed to IP world-building (sonic identity, extend the world)
  • Playbook: launch a fictional artist as real (ecosystem → fan club → staged signing → single → breakout)
  • Bridge adjacent fandoms (readers → viewers → listeners)
  • Experiential and brands go heavy
DRoster RenewalNewLaufey — "we know where your ceiling is"
  • Data-led thesis in three beats: what's working → what's missing (no Top 200 track ever; 16x radio gap) → the RCA plan
  • Celebrate first, then diagnose — "…But Something's Missing" lands because momentum came first
  • Heavy on Radio headroom, Commerce ("Build Fast, Land Loud"), Premium Content, Creative world-building
  • Publicity runs "More With Less"
