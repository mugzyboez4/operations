Parking & Transportation

Visitors
VISITORS are instructed to park In the Overland parking structure, Level 5 and above.
Employees
EMPLOYEE Parking is assigned based on organizational level and approved eligibility requirements.
  • Access is not determined by individual preference or observation of other employees
  • Employees not assigned to Overland Structure are assigned to Motor Gate parking
  • Exceptions may be approved for verified medical accommodations through PX
  • On the weekends, everyone has access to park in the Overland parking structure
Parking Assignements Are By Title

Directors = Level P2 & AboveOverland Parking Structure

  • Parking team manages EV charging rotation

  • Team rotates vehicles as chargers become available
  • Overland Charger Location: Level 1 and Level 11
