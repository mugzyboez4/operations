The plan

Interim, and what end of August looks like

We are not recommending moving everyone over. Not because it failed — because the access point is likely to change, and moving people twice is worse than moving them once. Here is what we do in the meantime.

Now → end of August

Scheduling moves as soon as Terenia says go. Old base stays authoritative for creative tickets, budget and updates. Historical campaign data gets staged, ready to load the day write access lands. Nobody new gets onboarded.

The ask that unlocks the month

Write access. Everything above is sequencing; this one is binary. With it, the end-of-August story is “our data is in and here is the plan for people.” Without it, it is “still waiting.”

What the end state probably is

Airtable as the database, fed by internal systems through the pilot, with a purpose-built front end on top that most people actually use. Mike Jang has floated two versions of exactly this — and has since named the tools: Softr and Lovable, both of which sit over Airtable. It means fewer seats, not more — which also answers the $840 problem.

The reason none of this hurts us. Budget, creative ticketing and scheduling were all designed platform-agnostic. That looked like extra work at the time. It means the environment can change underneath us and the builds still stand. We are dependent on having a system; we are not dependent on this one.
