Draft two

Finishing the deadline tracker

This is the one thing that gets checked off. It is already close, which is exactly why it should be the one — the goal is a finished thing in people’s hands, not a better thing still in progress.

The whole scope is three passes and a send. Anything that shows up along the way that isn’t one of these three things goes on a v2 list and does not delay the ship.

  1. Verify every date

    The dates weren’t right the first time we looked. Each deadline gets checked against the source it came from and the source gets named next to it, so the next person doesn’t have to re-derive it. Where a date is a house rule rather than a hard vendor cutoff, say so.

    Meg + Leo · the brain is shared, the deliveries differ

  2. One simplicity pass

    Jordan’s note on the first version was that it was good and needed tweaks. Read it as someone who has never seen it: can they find their date in under ten seconds without asking what a column means. Cut anything that fails that test.

    Meg · one pass, not four

  3. Send it to everybody

    Not a preview, not a “let me know if this looks right.” A finished thing with a line saying what it is, who it’s for, and where it lives. Feedback is welcome and goes into v2 — it does not reopen v1.

    Meg · this month
