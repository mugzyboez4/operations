03
How Work Comes In

Creative is triggered by the Campaign Lead. Nothing starts without a ticket and a brief.

A Campaign Lead owns the campaign — strategy, budget, timeline — and opens a ticket with the brief. That ticket lands with the Creative Manager (production work) or Tickets & Routing (design requests), who confirms deliverables, budget envelope and timeline before any spend. From there it moves through the processes below.
