Your work

Four responsibilities, plus reference workflows

These four make up most of your week — assets are the through-line in all of them. Open any one for the detail — and remember, if a request is missing information, asking for it up front is part of the job.

▸ 01 Creative ticket support Routing and follow-up
What comes in
  • Cover art updates
  • Social assets
  • Animated artwork
  • Marketing materials
  • Visual revisions
What you do
  • Check the request has what it needs
  • Route the ticket to the right team
  • Track status
  • Follow up on overdue items
  • Circle back to the requestor with updates
▸ 02 Pre-save support Links, assets, completion
You’ll help with
  • Pre-save launch requests
  • Link verification
  • Asset collection
  • Tracking completion

Most setups follow the same standard process, with the occasional artist-specific tweak.

▸ 03 Asset delivery & organization Final files, naming, distribution
What you handle
  • Collect final approved files from Creative
  • Name and file them to the standard structure
  • Log them in the Airtable asset log
  • Deliver to the teams that need them
  • Confirm receipt — delivery isn’t done until someone has it
Before an asset moves

Check it’s the approved version, the right spec for its destination, and named so the next person can find it without asking.

▸ 04 Meeting support Notes and action items
You may be asked to
  • Capture notes
  • Track action items
  • Monitor follow-ups
  • Log asset requests that come out of the meeting
Worth remembering

When an action gets assigned in a meeting, note who owns it and keep it tracked until it’s done.

Reference — how the recurring work flows

You won’t need these on day 1. They’re here for when a request lands and you’d like a checklist.

▸ A Asset requests & approvals What to gather, what to watch
Gather on every request
  • Artist name
  • Asset type
  • Due date
  • Intended usage
  • Approvals required

Friendly questions to ask: Is this approved? Who signs off? Do we have artwork yet? Is there video available?

Approvals are where most delays come from, so it helps to keep an eye on all three lanes at once: creative, management, and marketing.

A good habit

Check in before you have to — don’t assume an approval is on its way.

When to escalate

Before responding or escalating, ask three questions, in order:

01
Do I know the answer?

If yes — go ahead and respond.

02
Can I find the answer?

If yes — have a look first, then reply.

03
Does it touch a release date, artist commitment, budget, or public-facing asset?

If yes — bring it to Tarek. Latrice or Ryan can help you get there.

▸ B Visitors & parking Guest passes, structures, EV
Registering a guest
  1. Open the Sony visitor registration site (Traction Guest).
  2. Location group: Culver City. Then select Overland Structure.
  3. Create invite — add name, email, date and time.
  4. CC yourself so you keep a copy.

Guests get an email with a QR code for security, and park in the Overland structure, Level 5 and above.

Employee parking
  • Assigned by organizational level and approved eligibility.
  • Managers and below — Motor structure.
  • Assoc Directors and Directors — Overland, P2 and above.
  • VPs and SVPs — Overland, P1 and basement.
  • Weekends — everyone can use Overland.
  • Medical accommodations go through PX.

EV chargers: Overland Level 1 (3) and Level 11 (2), Motor Level 1 (5). The parking team rotates cars as chargers free up. Car wash: Motor Level 3, valet style.
