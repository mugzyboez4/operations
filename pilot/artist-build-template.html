Build notes
→Two columns: sections stack in the main column, the feed is sticky and spans the full page height. Header through Outstanding, plus the feed, are above the fold and load first; Trending, Assets and Detail Sections can lazy-load.
→Only visual assets link out to Air. Updates, notes and outstanding items stay in Airtable.
→No business-unit or internal-accounting fields anywhere on the page.
→Corners are square throughout — 0px radius, no rounded cards. Cream #F5F2EB background, Inter, hairline borders.
→Type tags are small, uppercase and color-coded by type — consistent across Latest, the feed and Assets.
→Net-new vs. the pilot today: Roster Group + Phase on Artists · extra social links (SPOTIFY_URI / Spotify ID already exist) · physical / video slices in the Releases.Type formula · "Show in feed" flag · Trending Data beyond the Spotify Followers / Popularity already on Artists. Everything else maps to fields already in the RCA US Pilot base — mostly a reorder of the existing artist record page.
