What's here so far

Two working prototypes.

Each started with a question someone asked. Neither is finished.

AD Tagging
Open →
The question: when someone needs Audience Development for an artist, who do they call?
Click any artist. The AD specialists tagged for that artist's genre and audience light up, with the matching tags showing why. The current roster is in there — 115 artists, every active person.
Missing Lifestyle tags on most of the lifestyle creators are empty; matching quality depends on the tags being filled in and correct.
Creator Database
Open →
The question: when we need a creator — a critic, a fan account, someone doing tour content — who do we know, what do they cost, who reps them?
Every creator from the UGC master doc, in one place. Filterable by function, size, genre, lifestyle tag, price band. Same tag vocabulary as the AD tool, so the two systems can eventually connect.

What's missing

Known gaps.

These are not on a roadmap yet. They're listed so the gaps are visible — each one needs knowledge or data from outside Campaign Ops.

Real audience data

Tags are a first pass at audience indexing. Actual data — who follows whom, what they listen to, how they engage — lives in Modash, Chartmetric, HypeAuditor, Cobrand. Hooking those in is a budget conversation and an integration project.

Past campaign performance

Which creators showed up for which artists. Which posts moved the needle. Which collabs converted to streams. This data exists, but it lives across decks and screenshots and people's heads. Pulling it into one place is its own project.

The things that can't be tagged

Taste. Trust. The reason a particular creator won't post for a particular artist even though every tag says they should. The reason a publication runs your story and another one doesn't. These don't fit in a dropdown; they're knowledge that lives with people.

How to actually help

How to contribute.

There's now one place to put all of this — the board. A wrong tag, a missing creator, or an idea for a tool — post it there and the whole team can see it.

  • If a tag is wrong Flag it — the data lives in Airtable and changes are easy. A creator that shouldn't be Pop Influencers, an artist that should be LGBTQ Culture.
  • If something's missing A tag category that should exist, a creator who isn't there, an artist who's been overlooked — flag it the same way.
  • If you want to add something Notes on which creator worked for which artist. A brand conflict you know about. A relationship that matters. Anything useful to others — send it.
  • If you have an idea for the next tool If there's something that would help your team's work, describe it and Campaign Ops will look at building it.

Corrections and ideas via the board or directly to Campaign Ops.
