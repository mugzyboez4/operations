Finance

Finance's rules of the road

From Sony finance's Console PO requirements
  • PO description = Artist + single/album + date(s) of service. Due date = expected date of service.
  • Pro-rate everything. Each deliverable gets its own category line — artwork vs video, TV vs other. Lumping has artist-cap implications.
  • Blanket POs when one budget spans multiple vendors or front/back payments — shoots, tour support, retainers.
  • Attachments by type: tour support → approved budget · official video → VRA · T&E → receipts + pre-approval.

Why the buckets matter

Category + phase → budget groups
  • Every row lands in a finance bucket against a budget phase, so actuals are analyzable the moment they're captured.
  • Actuals roll up into the five roster groups — G1 Flagship through G5 Emerging — the uniform tiering behind budget reviews.
  • That gives every budget conversation a baseline to point at, instead of a personal judgment call.
